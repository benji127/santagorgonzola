Our family story

Santa Gorgonzola is a family start-up. We began it as a small business to get our daughter excited about making things, so she can craft these ideas with us and see them become real celebrations for real people.

Every piece is designed, cut, folded and glued by hand in Canada: birthday numbers, name letters, charcuterie boxes and Christmas shapes. When you order from us, you’re helping a young maker learn her craft.

FAQ

Where do you ship?

We ship nationwide across Canada, and local pick-up is available.

Can I choose colours and sizes?

Yes. Tell us the colours, sizes and date when you place a custom order.

How long does an order take?

Every piece is handmade, so order early for big events. Tell us your date and we’ll confirm what’s possible.
